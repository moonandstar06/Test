## About story (Settings → About)

> Bramble & Brain Co. started with a drawer full of abandoned planners.
>
> We'd bought the beautiful ones, the bestselling ones, the ones influencers swore fixed their lives — and every single one ended up shoved in a drawer within three weeks. Not because we didn't want to be organized. Because every system assumed a brain that doesn't lose its train of thought mid-sentence, doesn't need five reminders to remember reminders exist, and doesn't treat "just be disciplined" as an actual plan.
>
> So we stopped looking for the right planner and started building one — from the actual research on ADHD executive function, and from real feedback from other neurodivergent people testing early versions.
>
> Every system we sell is designed around how ADHD and neurodivergent brains actually work: visual over abstract, low-friction over feature-heavy, dopamine-aware instead of guilt-based. Time blindness gets a real calendar view instead of a to-do list that assumes you know what day it is. Motivation gets a menu instead of a lecture. Bad days get a "low-spoons mode" instead of getting you kicked out of the system entirely.
>
> We're not a productivity brand that added an ADHD tag. We built this for brains like ours, first.

## FAQ (Settings → Policies → Additional shop policies / FAQ)

**Do I need a paid Notion account to use these templates?**
No — Notion's free plan covers everything in our templates. You'll just need a free Notion account to duplicate the template into your own workspace.

**What file formats do I get?**
Depends on the listing — check the format details on each listing page. Options include a Notion template share-link, a print-ready PDF, and a GoodNotes/iPad-compatible hyperlinked PDF. Bundles include multiple formats.

**Will this work on my phone/Android/iPad?**
Notion templates work on any device with the free Notion app (iOS, Android, desktop, web). Printable PDFs print from any device. GoodNotes-formatted files are built for iPad + Apple Pencil apps like GoodNotes or Notability.

**Can I get a refund?**
Digital files can't be refunded once downloaded, but if something's actually wrong — wrong format, broken file, checkout error — message us and we'll make it right immediately. See our Returns & Exchanges policy for details.

**How does personalization work?**
After checkout, send your personalization details (name, color palette, or whatever the listing asks for) through Etsy Messages. Turnaround is within 48 hours, usually faster.

**Can I use this for my business or clients?**
These are for personal use only — please don't resell, redistribute, or use our templates as the base for your own product. Want a commercial-use license? Message us to talk about it.
