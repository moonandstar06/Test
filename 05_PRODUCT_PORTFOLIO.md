# Initial Product Portfolio — Bramble & Brain Co.

20 listings at launch (within the 15-30 target), architected so each format
(Notion / GoodNotes digital planner / printable PDF) is built once per module
and sold three ways — that's the automation leverage: one design pass, three
SKUs.

## HERO PRODUCTS (discovery + flagship differentiation)

1. **The ADHD Life OS — Notion Template** ($35) — full system: task capture,
   time-blindness-aware calendar view, dopamen-menu module, body-doubling
   session tracker. This is the flagship; SEO and ad budget concentrate here first.
2. **The ADHD Life OS — Digital Planner (GoodNotes/iPad)** ($32) — same system,
   handwriting-first format for the large non-Notion buyer segment.
3. **The ADHD Life OS — Printable PDF Edition** ($22) — paper-first version,
   captures buyers who explicitly don't want another screen.

## Strategic purpose summary

- Every listing maps to a named role above — nothing is in the portfolio "just
  because." If a listing can't state its role, it doesn't launch.
- Modules 4-9 are reused as bundle components (13-15) and as the base for
  personalization (16-17) — one production pass, five monetization paths.
- Experiments 18-20 are deliberately single-SKU and cheap to produce; a winner
  gets promoted to its own hero/profit/entry set next cycle, a loser gets
  killed per the criteria in `03_UNIT_ECONOMICS.md` without ceremony.

## Production specs (all digital, applies to every SKU above)

- Source files built in Canva Pro (brand kit locked to `04_BRAND.md` palette/type) and Notion (duplicate-link template)
- Deliverable formats: PDF (print + GoodNotes/iPad hyperlinked-tabs version), Notion share link
- Every file watermark-free, buyer-ready, with a 1-page "how to use" cover sheet
- AI disclosure: if any AI image/design tool touches a deliverable, disclose per `07_OPERATIONS_SOP.md` — default plan uses original Canva-built design elements, not AI-generated imagery, specifically to avoid the disclosure/enforcement risk documented in that file
- QC checklist before any listing goes live: opens correctly in Notion + Adobe Reader + GoodNotes, no broken links, no leftover Lorem Ipsum/placeholder text, palette matches brand kit
