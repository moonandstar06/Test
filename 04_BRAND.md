# Brand Strategy

## Positioning statement

For neurodivergent adults and parents of neurodivergent kids who have tried
and abandoned generic productivity systems, [Brand] makes digital planning
systems designed around how ADHD/autistic brains actually work — not
willpower-shaming productivity culture — so that "staying organized" stops
feeling like one more thing you're failing at.

## Target customer personas

1. **"Overwhelmed Overachiever" (28-42)** — diagnosed or self-identified adult
   ADHD, has bought 3+ planners that didn't stick, active in ADHD TikTok/
   Instagram communities, will pay premium for something that "gets it."
2. **"The Systems-Builder Parent" (30-45)** — parent of an ND child, wants
   visual schedules/routines for the kid and increasingly for themselves too;
   entry point into the autism/homeschool expansion line.
3. **"Notion-Curious Beginner"** — has heard Notion can help but is
   intimidated by blank-page setup; wants a done-for-them system, not a
   template requiring assembly.

## Tone of voice

Warm, direct, zero shame. Never "just be more disciplined" language. Uses
real ADHD/ND community vocabulary (spoons, body doubling, time blindness,
dopamine menu) accurately and respectfully — never as decoration. Humor is
allowed; condescension is not.

## Shop name candidates (collision-checked via web search for existing Etsy
shops/trademarks with identical names — no exact conflicts found; verify
again at registration since availability changes)

1. **Bramble & Brain Co.** — warm, distinctive, not literal about ADHD (broad
   appeal to anyone who wants the system without the label)
2. **LoopWorks Studio** — nods to routines/loops without clinical language
3. **Softshell Systems** — "soft" = kind to yourself, "shell" = structure/
   protection; slightly abstract but ownable
4. **Recommended: Bramble & Brain Co.** — most memorable, easiest to say
   aloud, works as a shop name and a future non-Etsy domain
   (bramblebrain.co / bramblebrain.com — verify availability at registration).

## Visual identity direction

- **Color system:** warm terracotta + sage green + soft cream base, one
  saturated accent (marigold) for CTAs/highlights — calm but not clinical,
  avoids the cold blue/gray "corporate productivity app" look competitors default to.
- **Typography:** rounded, friendly sans-serif for body (e.g. a Quicksand/
  Nunito-family font) + a slightly playful display face for headers — legible
  first, personality second (dyslexia/ADHD-friendly readability matters to this audience).
- **Logo direction:** simple lockup, no literal brain/puzzle-piece imagery
  (overused and, for puzzle-piece specifically, associated with a controversial
  autism-org symbol some ND adults actively dislike — avoid).
- **Photography/mockup direction:** lifestyle flat-lays on a desk with warm
  lighting, screens showing the digital product in actual use (device
  mockups), never sterile white-background product shots only.

## About/story direction

Origin story frames the brand as built *from* the ND community's stated
frustrations with existing planners (grounded in the actual buyer-language
evidence in `02_MARKET_RESEARCH_NICHE_MATRIX.md`), not as a generic "we love
organizing" founder story.

## Policies stance

Clear, generous digital-goods policy stated up front (no-refund-after-download
is standard for digital but we will offer free format swaps/fixes and
goodwill exceptions — see `09_CUSTOMER_RESPONSE_LIBRARY.md`), processing time
stated conservatively and beaten in practice, personalization turnaround
committed and tracked.
