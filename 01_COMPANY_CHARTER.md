# Company Charter — Etsy Micro-Commerce Co.

Founded: 2026-08-20 | Owner/Investor: chari.delacruz@gmail.com | CEO: Claude (this session)

## Operating model reality check

This "company" runs as one CEO session (me) that, when parallel research is useful,
delegates one-shot research tasks to sub-agents playing each functional role below.
Sub-agents have **no persistent memory of their own** — they don't exist as standing
employees between tasks. Continuity lives in this directory, not in agent memory.
Every session that picks this project back up should read `00_EXECUTION_LEDGER.md`
first.

Tools actually available: WebSearch, WebFetch (etsy.com is blocked by egress proxy —
no direct Etsy browsing), file/shell access, sub-agent spawning. **Not** available:
Etsy Seller API/MCP, any payment or banking tool, browser automation, ad-platform
API. This caps the company at "fully-prepared, human-clicks-launch" rather than
"fully autonomous storefront."

## Roles

- **CSFO (Strategy & Finance)** — demand/competitor/keyword research, unit economics,
  kill/scale calls. Skeptical by design; requires evidence before any resourcing.
- **Head of Product** — concepts, differentiation, production specs, collections.
  Originality required; zero tolerance for trademark/character/logo infringement.
- **COO (Operations & Fulfillment)** — shop config, listings, SOPs, policy compliance.
- **CGO (Growth & Marketing)** — SEO, content, ads; optimizes for contribution
  profit, not impressions.
- **Head of CX** — message templates, escalation rules, voice-of-customer → Product.

## Decision rights

CEO breaks ties, sets priorities, and is the only role allowed to greenlight spend
against the $250 budget. No product launches without clearing the profitability
gate in `03_UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`. No listing goes live without passing the IP/policy
screen in `07_OPERATIONS_SOP.md`.
