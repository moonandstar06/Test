# Marketing Engine

## Phase 1 (weeks 1-2): organic only, no ad spend

- Reason: need baseline conversion data per SKU before any paid spend is
  rational (can't calculate real CAC/ROAS against guesses).
- Pinterest: 3-5 pins/week per hero+profit listing, standard product pins
  linking directly to the listing. Content templates (reusable, not
  bespoke per pin): "before/after" system chaos-to-calm graphic, "what's
  inside" flatlay, quote-card using real buyer-language pain points from
  `02_MARKET_RESEARCH_NICHE_MATRIX.md`.
- Instagram/TikTok: repeatable content templates —
  (1) "POV: you finally found a planner that gets it" trend format,
  (2) module walkthrough screen-recording,
  (3) dopamine-menu explainer (this concept is inherently shareable —
      prioritize it as the top-of-funnel piece),
  (4) satisfied-customer-style day-in-the-life using the system (staged by
      us, clearly, never fabricated as a testimonial).
- No fake reviews, no fabricated testimonials, ever — Etsy reviews only get
  cited once they're real.

## Phase 2 (week 3+): controlled Etsy Ads test, gated by Phase 1 data

- Only fund campaigns for SKUs that already show organic favorites/conversion
  signal — don't advertise a cold, unproven listing.
- Test budget: from the $150 ad reserve in `03_UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`, deployed
  in $25 tranches per SKU, not all at once.
- Targets restated: CAC ceiling $15 target / $20 hard stop-loss, break-even
  CAC $30.10 on the $35 hero bundle (see `03_UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`).
- Weekly review of CAC/ROAS per campaign; kill anything over stop-loss same
  day it's detected, don't wait for the weekly review to act on an obvious breach.

## Offsite Ads (Etsy-run, not seller-controlled)

Below the $10,000/yr threshold this is optional — leave it **off** at launch;
revisit once trailing revenue approaches the threshold, since past it we're
enrolled automatically and it changes the margin math in `03_UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`.

## Content calendar cadence

Weekly: 3-5 Pinterest pins, 2-3 short-form video posts, 1 new-listing or
restock announcement if applicable. Monthly: 1 seasonal bundle push (aligned
to portfolio items #14-15), 1 content series themed around a specific ND
pain point sourced from CX's voice-of-customer notes (see
`09_CUSTOMER_RESPONSE_LIBRARY.md`).
